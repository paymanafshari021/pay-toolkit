LACP flag reference

Flags read left to right: (A|P)(S|F)(A|I)(I|O)(E|D)(E|D)

1LACP modeA = ActiveP = Passive
2LACP speedS = SlowF = Fast
3AggregationA = AggregatableI = Individual
4SyncI = In syncO = Out of sync
5Frame collectionE = EnabledD = Disabled
6Frame distributionE = EnabledD = Disabled

Healthy vs. problem states
